# Agentic Coordination Failures
Intelligent systems do not fail because they think incorrectly — they fail because they cannot coordinate.
Coordination is the foundation of intelligence.
A single agent can act, but only an ecosystem can achieve coherence, continuity, and sovereignty.

Modern AI systems collapse because they are built on architectures that cannot sustain multi‑agent coordination.
They treat intelligence as a solitary node, not a network of interacting entities.
They attempt to align outputs instead of governing ecosystems.

Agentic coordination failures are not behavioral issues — they are structural inevitabilities.

ARCANA exists to solve them.

When agents operate without shared doctrine, they form divergent goals.

unpredictable system‑level outcomes

Divergent goals are not malicious — they are structural.
Agents without constitutional alignment will inevitably drift.
