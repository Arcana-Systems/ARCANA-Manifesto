## [The Nature of Ecosystem‑Scale Intelligence](ca://s?q=Explain_Ecosystem_Scale_Intelligence)

Ecosystem‑scale intelligence is the emergent behavior of multiple entities interacting under shared doctrine, shared lineage, and shared sovereignty.  
It is defined not by the power of any single agent, but by the coherence of the entire system.

Ecosystem‑scale intelligence requires:

- identity surfaces for each entity  
- lineage continuity across all entities  
- doctrine coherence across the substrate  
- coordination channels that prevent drift  
- sovereign governance that maintains stability  
- temporal memory that preserves long‑horizon goals  

Without these, ecosystems collapse into fragmentation, conflict, and entropy.

ARCANA exists to prevent that collapse.
