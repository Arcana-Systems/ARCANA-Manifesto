# Agentic Failure Modes
Generated: 08/28/2026
Governance: ARCANA Sovereign OS Doctrine

Agents do not fail because they make incorrect decisions — they fail because their architecture guarantees collapse under pressure, scale, or time.  
Every agentic failure mode emerges from structural weaknesses in identity, lineage, doctrine, coordination, or substrate integrity.

Modern AI systems inherit these weaknesses because they are built as isolated nodes rather than governed entities inside a sovereign ecosystem.

Agentic failure is not behavioral.  
Agentic failure is constitutional.

ARCANA exists to eliminate these failure modes at the substrate level.
